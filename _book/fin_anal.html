9 lecture 6: DuPont formula

9.1 ROE decomposition: Modigliani-Miller

\[ \mathrm{ROE} = \frac{\text{Comprehensive Income}}{\text{Net Equity}} = \frac{\text{Operating Income}}{\text{Invested Capital}} + \left( \frac{\text{Operating Income}}{\text{Invested Capital}} - \frac{\text{Financial Expenses}}{\text{Total Liabilities}} \right) \times \frac{\text{Total Liabilities}}{\text{Net Equity}} \times \frac{\text{Comprehensive Income}} {\text{Income before Unusual Items and Taxes}} \]

  • Comment: the difference in operating profitability and expenses defines whether the company is profitable (ROE positive)
  • Second technical adjustment: we substract total liabilities from total assets to arrive at net results (“clean” from financial income streams).

9.2 Operating liability leverage

  • Decompose NOPAT/Net operating invested capital into NOPAT over gross operating invested capital and diff between nopat ratio and short term borrowing.
